About The Department
Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering was established in 1964 to offer Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electronics & Communication and Master of Engineering degrees in (a) Advanced Electronics and (b) Applied Electronics & Servomechanisms. In 1968, the postgraduate programme was restructured with specializations in the areas of Communication Systems, Control and guidance, Microwaves and Radar, and Solid State Electronics. Two new undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in Computer Science and Technology were started in the year 1982. Keeping in view the activities of the Department, the name was changed to Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering. In January 2013, a separate Department of Computer Science and Engineering was carved out, and the department was again renamed as Electronics and Communication Engineering. At present, the department has 40 full-time faculty members and offers one B. Tech. program in Electronics & Communication Engineering and five full-time M. Tech. programs viz. M. Tech. in Microelectronics & VLSI (MeV), Communication Systems, RF & Microwaves (RFM) Engineering, THz Communication & Sensing (TCS), and Semiconductor Technology. The Department is also offering a separate M.Tech program specially designed for professionals working in the area of VLSI.

• ECE Dept, IIT Roorkee, extends its heartiest congratulations to Ms. Urvashi, Ph.D. Scholar, and Prof. Saravana Kumar Manivannan on their selection among the winners of the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship (QIF) India 2026 for the project “Hybrid Pipelined ADC Design for Wideband Applications.”

• Congratulations to Sumit Gupta, M.Tech student, ECE, IIT Roorkee, for receiving the Best Paper Award at VDAT 2026 held at JIIT Noida. His award-winning research focuses on RF and signal integrity analysis of Through-Glass Via (TGV) structures. The work was carried out under the guidance of Prof. Sanjeev Kumar Manhas. Wishing him continued success! 🏆👏

• ECE Dept, IIT Roorkee, congratulates Prof. Vinod Pankajashan on receiving the Excellence in Teaching Award for the third time, recognising his 15 years of dedicated contribution to teaching, mentorship, and academic excellence at IIT Roorkee. 🏆🎉

• ECE, IIT Roorkee, in collaboration with CSDT, hosted a Distinguished Alumni Talk by Dr. Charan Gurumurthy, Senior Vice President, Tata Electronics Pvt. Ltd., on “Semiconductor Chiplet Heterogeneous Integration Solutions” on 14 August 2026, from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM., ECE Conference. The talk provided valuable insights into chiplet architectures, heterogeneous integration, and emerging semiconductor technologies, drawing on Dr. Gurumurthy’s extensive industry experience.

• The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, IIT Roorkee, proudly congratulates Prof. Amalendu Patnaik on being awarded the prestigious J. C. Bose Grant (2026) by the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF). This esteemed recognition honours his exceptional contributions to research, innovation, and academic excellence.

• The C-DOT Centre of Excellence (CoE) in Telecom, ECE Deptt. IIT Roorkee, successfully organized the workshop "Bridging Academia and Industry for 6G and Beyond" on 28 July 2026. The workshop brought together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, scientists, faculty members, and students to strengthen academia–industry collaboration in next-generation communication technologies. The event featured expert sessions by Shri Atul Kumar Gupta, Shri Amit Kumar Karna, Ms. Shivani Arora, and Ms. Alka Kumar , providing a valuable platform for knowledge sharing, networking, and fostering research and innovation in advanced communication technologies.
