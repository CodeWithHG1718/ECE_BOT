Research Interests
Physical layer design and analysis for RF and optical wireless communication systems, including but not limited to the following areas:, Advanced Multiple-Access Techniques (NOMA and RSMA), Advanced Modulation Systems (SM and OAM Modulation), Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces-Assisted Systems, Visible Light Communication, MIMO Systems, Full Duplex (FD) System, Free-Space Quantum Communication, and Experiments and design of optical wireless communication Systems
